Every department of CAC Good Works Assembly exists to fulfil God's mandate.

Family Ministry

Building Godly Homes

"Unless the Lord builds the house, the builders labour in vain." — Psalm 127:1

The Family Ministry at CAC Good Works Assembly is dedicated to strengthening marriages, equipping parents and building Christ-centred homes that honour God and bless communities.

Through regular couples meetings (Ruth & Boaz), marriage enrichment sessions, parenting workshops and family counselling, we walk alongside families in every season of life.

Teenage Church

Igniting the Next Generation

"Don't let anyone look down on you because you are young." — 1 Timothy 4:12

The Teenage Church is a dynamic, Spirit-filled space designed specifically for young people aged 13–19. Here, teens encounter God authentically, develop their gifts and grow in leadership.

Through high-energy worship, relevant Bible teaching, mentoring programs and youth-led events, we raise a generation that is bold, purposeful and deeply rooted in Christ.

Children Church

Training Up Champions

"Train up a child in the way he should go; even when he is old he will not depart from it." — Proverbs 22:6

Our Children Church is a safe, fun and spiritually enriching environment for children ages 3–12. With creative, age-appropriate Bible lessons, worship songs and interactive activities, we help every child build a lifelong relationship with Jesus.

Our trained and passionate children's workers are committed to making God's Word come alive in the heart of every child.
