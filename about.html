About CAC Good Works

Rooted in faith. Driven by purpose. Growing in God's presence.

Our Story

A Church Born in Prayer

CAC Good Works Assembly is a vibrant, Spirit-filled congregation of the Christ Apostolic Church, founded on an unwavering conviction that God desires to manifest His glory in the midst of His people. Since our founding, we have been a community of believers passionately pursuing God's presence and transforming lives through the power of the gospel.

Under the anointed leadership of Pastor Akin Akinbajo and Dr. Mrs Busola Akinbajo, the assembly has grown into a dynamic community with ministries spanning family, youth, children and community outreach — all rooted in the Word of God and fuelled by fervent prayer.

Our mission remains unchanged: This is a church that calls people to seek God's manifest presence, perfecting the saints towards Christ like nature and creating a friendly atmosphere for people to worship, locate and achieve their God's giving destiny... Where we raise the Josephs, Daniels and Esthers of our time.

What We Believe

Our Core Beliefs

The Holy Scriptures

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible Word of God — our ultimate authority for faith, doctrine and daily life.

Salvation through Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is the only Son of God who died for our sins and rose again. Salvation is by grace alone through faith alone in Christ alone.

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the baptism of the Holy Spirit with the evidence of speaking in tongues, empowering believers for life and ministry.

Prayer & Fasting

We believe in the transformative power of prayer and fasting as the foundation of every spiritual breakthrough and victory.

The Church

We believe the Church is the body of Christ — called to worship God, make disciples, love one another and serve the world.

Second Coming

We believe in the imminent, physical return of Jesus Christ to establish His eternal kingdom and reign over all creation.
